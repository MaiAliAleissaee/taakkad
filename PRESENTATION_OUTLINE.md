# Five-minute hackathon presentation outline

1. **Problem (30 seconds):** impersonated institutions and familiar-looking channels can make phishing requests appear trustworthy. Present this as a problem statement, not an invented prevalence statistic.
2. **Solution (40 seconds):** Taakkad connects private public checks, independent organization/channel accreditation and restricted investigation review. Arabic-first, bilingual and human-review-centered.
3. **Differentiation (35 seconds):** registry facts are distinct from AI interpretations; shared-indicator graphs are leads, never offender attribution. Approval and publication require independent governance.
4. **Live demonstration (2 minutes):** approved fictional channel -> suspicious Arabic request with real AI -> linked documentation IP -> restricted Analyst graph -> separate Officer workflow. Show access denial when the wrong institutional account opens a dashboard.
5. **Architecture and AI (35 seconds):** server-side OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol, double redaction, grounded extraction, private component-local results, atomic visitor/global limits and honest fallback. No calibrated fraud probability claims.
6. **Security/governance (25 seconds):** secure password login, separate roles, live authorization, row-level policies, private documents/audit and approved-only public projections. Neither demo staff account has Supervisor privileges.
7. **Impact/future (15 seconds):** clearer decision support and a path to reviewed integrations. Future domain ownership checks, authenticated email signals and consented threat intelligence are proposals only.

Close honestly: fictional hackathon prototype, not a deployed police service. Do not invent partnerships, government approval, detection accuracy, performance metrics or operational enforcement.
