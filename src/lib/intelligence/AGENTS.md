# Proactive intelligence boundaries
- Require authenticated Analyst role and the existing active-session predicate on all intelligence reads/commands; role labels and preview tokens never bypass session revocation.
- Keep records per-user in Cloud and save evidence only through the service-only optimistic commit after caller authorization; browser imports cannot provide DNS/RDAP observations.
- Reuse bounded fixed-origin metadata helpers with bare registrable hostnames; never fetch source links or candidate websites, and never infer registration from DNS.
- Preserve generated/imported/synthetic and independently observed facts as distinct states; private early warnings are human-review priorities, not fraud verdicts or public registry approvals.
- Correlate only current accessible reports with type-aware normalized exact indicator matches; seeded matches remain synthetic and relationships never attribute an offender.
